About GlobalNews

GlobalNews is an independent news discovery terminal designed to help readers scan the world quickly and then reach the original publisher for the full report.

What we do

  • Collect headlines and short feed-provided excerpts from international publishers.
  • Normalize timestamps, source metadata, categories and geographic signals.
  • Remove duplicate feed entries and rank stories by relevance and freshness.
  • Link readers directly to the original publisher URL.

What we do not do

GlobalNews does not mirror full articles, bypass paywalls, impersonate publishers, or present third-party reporting as GlobalNews reporting.

Editorial position

Our product goal is signal over noise: clear attribution, transparent sourcing, fast discovery and respect for original journalism.

Technology

The site is a lightweight static frontend backed by an automated RSS/Atom collection pipeline and scheduled GitHub Actions. The project is intentionally dependency-light.
