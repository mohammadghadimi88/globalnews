Privacy

Current status: GlobalNews does not intentionally run analytics or behavioral tracking by default, and advertising is disabled.

What the current site does

The site loads public news data and static application assets. Your browser may necessarily provide standard technical information to the hosting provider, such as an IP address and request metadata, as part of serving web pages.

Future advertising

If advertising or analytics are enabled later, this policy will be updated before those technologies are deployed. Depending on the service and visitor location, consent management, cookies and regional privacy requirements may apply.

Third-party publishers

When you click an external news link, you leave GlobalNews and are subject to that publisher's privacy policy and technologies.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact page.
