Editorial Policy

GlobalNews is a discovery and aggregation service, not a replacement for the original newsroom.

Source attribution

Every story identifies its publisher and links to the original article. Headlines, dates and brief excerpts are retained only to make discovery possible.

Selection

Sources are selected for geographic diversity, reputation, editorial relevance and machine-readable feeds. Source inclusion does not mean GlobalNews endorses every individual report or viewpoint.

Automation

Collection, normalization, categorization and duplicate detection are automated. Automated classification can be imperfect. When a source, category or location is wrong, we prefer correction over concealment.

Corrections and removals

Publishers can request source removal or correction through the contact page. We will review requests and update the registry when appropriate.

Advertising and sponsorship

Advertising is currently disabled. If monetization is introduced, sponsored material will be clearly distinguishable from editorial discovery content and disclosed to readers.

Trust

We do not claim to be the original publisher of stories displayed through the service. The publisher remains the authoritative destination for the underlying report.
